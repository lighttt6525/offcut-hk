Offcut HK

Collect. Sort. Return.

Fabric that was never worn shouldn't go to landfill.

I have fabric I want to volunteer

We collect fabric samples, leftover sample yardage and unworn development samples from Hong Kong sourcing offices. Student volunteers sort it by hand at The Mills in Tsuen Wan. Anything over a metre goes to design students and makers. Everything else is stripped clean of cardboard and staples and sent to Novetex in Tai Po for mechanical recycling.

The case

Why reuse, not recycling

Recycling is the fallback, not the answer. Keeping fabric intact and in use beats breaking it back down every time.

Process

How it works

Five steps, from a box in a sourcing office to a report on your desk.

  1. 01

    Collect

    We arrange a pickup from your office. Boxes, bolts, sample rails — we take it as it is.

  2. 02

    Sort

    Student volunteers sort it by hand at The Mills in Tsuen Wan, by size and by fibre.

  3. 03

    Return

    Anything over a metre is listed on the materials board and goes to design students and makers.

  4. 04

    Recycle

    Everything else is stripped clean of cardboard and staples and sent to Novetex in Tai Po.

  5. 05

    Report

    You get a diversion report by weight, plus written confirmation that any logos were destroyed.

For brands & sourcing offices

I have fabric

If it was never worn and never sold, it shouldn't be paying for landfill space. Here is exactly what we can and can't accept.

Commercial destruction firms already charge you to shred this material. We do the same job cheaper, and the fabric survives.

We take

  • Fabric 1 metre or over
  • Sample yardage
  • Unworn development samples
  • Salesman samples
  • Trims and notions
  • Leather and lace
  • Yarn cones

We don't take

  • Worn clothing
  • Bedding
  • Curtains
  • Anything under an ATA Carnet
  • Wet or contaminated material

What you get back

A diversion report

Weight collected, weight returned to use, weight recycled — with dates, for your sustainability reporting.

A destruction certificate

Written confirmation that all logos, labels and branded trims were removed and destroyed before anything left the studio.

A volunteer day

A sorting session for your staff at The Mills — a straightforward team day with something to show for it.
