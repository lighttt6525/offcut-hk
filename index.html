Process

How it works

Five steps, from a box in a sourcing office to a report on your desk.

  1. 01

    Collect

    We arrange a pickup from your office. Boxes, bolts, sample rails — we take it as it is.

  2. 02

    Sort by hand

    Student volunteers separate fabric from cardboard headers and sort by size and fibre.

  3. 03

    Reuse

    Usable material is listed on the board and goes to design students, makers, NGOs and schools.

  4. 04

    Recycle the rest

    What can't be reused goes to mechanical recycling rather than to landfill.

  5. 05

    Report back

    You get a diversion report by weight, plus confirmation that any logos were destroyed.

Take

For brands & sourcing offices

I have fabric

If it was never worn and never sold, it shouldn't be paying for landfill space. Here's exactly what we can and can't accept.

We take

  • Fabric pieces 1 metre or over
  • Sample yardage
  • Unworn development samples
  • Salesman samples
  • Trims and notions
  • Leather and lace
  • Yarn cones

We don't take

  • Worn clothing
  • Bedding
  • Curtains
  • Anything under an ATA Carnet
  • Anything wet or contaminated

What you get back

A diversion report

Weight collected, weight returned to use, weight recycled — with dates, for your sustainability reporting.

A destruction certificate

Written confirmation that all logos, labels and branded trims were removed and destroyed before anything left the studio.

A volunteer day

A sorting session for your staff at our studio — a straightforward team day with something to show for it.
